Frequently Asked Questions

What if my package is lost?

If your package is lost before delivery, we will issue a full refund or send a replacement at no cost.

Can I return a product?

Yes, you can return any product within 14 days of receipt for a full refund, provided it is in original condition.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping typically takes 3–7 business days within Europe.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we ship to Germany, France, Spain, and Poland. Contact support for other regions.
