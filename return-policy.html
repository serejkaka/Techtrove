Return Policy

At TechTrove, we prioritize customer satisfaction. Our return policy is designed to protect your rights:

  • Lost Packages: If your package is lost before delivery, we offer a full refund or replacement.
  • 14-Day Returns: You can return any product within 14 days of receipt for a full refund, provided it is in original condition.
  • Easy Process: Contact our support team to initiate a return. We provide a prepaid shipping label for convenience.

For more details, visit our FAQ or contact support.
